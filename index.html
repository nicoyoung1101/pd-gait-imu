Preserving Clinically Relevant Gait Features in Wearable-Sensor Pose Estimation for Parkinson’s Disease

Kai Yang1, Haoyuan Sun1, Hiroyuki Hamada1, Ken Kikuchi1, Atsushi Yamashita1, Diego Torricelli2, and Qi An1,*

1The University of Tokyo  ·  2Centre for Automation and Robotics (CAR), Spanish National Research Council

Reconstruction video will appear here shortly.

Reference motion compared with pose-only (P) and clinical-aware (C) fine-tuning.

Take-home message

  • Clinical-aware fine-tuning reduced joint-velocity and leg-swing errors vs. pose-only fine-tuning.
  • The benefit held with a head-free five-IMU set, updating only 0.48% of parameters.

Main result

C − P (absolute log-ratio error)Six IMUsFive IMUs
Joint velocity−0.025−0.037
Sagittal leg swing−0.027−0.022

Negative values mean lower error with clinical-aware tuning. All four 95% confidence intervals exclude zero. Virtual IMUs from 781 walks of 23 people with Parkinson’s disease; subject-wise cross-validation.
